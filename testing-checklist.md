# Testing Checklist

Before publishing or sharing v1.0, test the extension on Chrome and Edge.

## Core checks

- Netflix loads normally.
- Skip Intro works.
- Skip Recap works.
- Next Episode is not clicked accidentally.
- Skip Credits is off by default.
- Debug Overlay is off by default.
- The extension does not pause playback.
- The extension does not skip to the next episode.
- The extension does not prevent the streaming site from loading.
- The extension does not click Play/Pause controls.

## Other supported sites

Where available, test episodes with visible skip buttons on:

- Disney+
- Hulu
- Max
- Prime Video
