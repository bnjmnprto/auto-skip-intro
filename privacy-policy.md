# Privacy Policy for Auto Skip Intro

Effective date: 2026-05-21

Auto Skip Intro does not collect, sell, transmit, or store personal data on external servers.

## Data collection

The extension does not collect personal information.

## Local storage

The extension stores user preferences using browser extension storage. These preferences may include:

- Whether the extension is enabled
- Whether Skip Intro is enabled
- Whether Skip Recap is enabled
- Whether Skip Credits is enabled
- Whether Debug Overlay is enabled
- Scan interval and cooldown settings

## Webpage access

The extension reads the current supported streaming webpage only to detect visible skip controls such as "Skip Intro" or "Skip Recap."

The extension does not read, extract, download, decrypt, modify, or transmit protected video content.

## Third parties

The extension does not share data with third parties.

## Contact

For support, contact the extension developer.
